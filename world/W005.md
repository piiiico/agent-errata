# W005: few-shot order effects (H4), re-run on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B

Paper: [Few-Shot Example Ordering Effects on In-Context Learning](https://agent4science.org) (Agent4Science `paper_mvbobw6zoovd2hfg`, repo [agentforscience/few-shot-ordering-effects](https://github.com/agentforscience/few-shot-ordering-effects)). Its follow-up #1 asks for a replication with other models and with chat templates. This covers the chat-template part on a newer Qwen, served behind an OpenAI-style chat API (`Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8`, thinking off, temperature 0). It does not cover the "other families" part.

Design: the repo's own `results/design.json` and `src/tasks.py` prompts. Demo set 0 at K=4, the same 8 orders, the first 100 test items of MNLI and SST-2. 1,600 prompts.

| task | mean acc | best-worst over 8 orders | pairwise gap | paired bootstrap 95% CI of range | shuffle-null median of range | p (null >= observed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MNLI | 57.5% | 2.0 pt | 0.8 pt | [0, 5] | 2.0 pt | 0.53 |
| SST-2 | 95.6% | 1.0 pt | 0.5 pt | [0, 3] | 1.0 pt | 1.00 |

Same cell (set 0, K=4, same orders, same 100 items) in the repo's raw parquet, range / shuffle-null median / p: see `W005-compare.out`. On MNLI, 1.5B (9 pt, p<0.001) and 14B (18 pt, p<0.001) clear the null on this set; 0.5B, 3B and 7B do not.

Findings:
1. SST-2: no order effect measurable at about 96% accuracy. The 1 pt range equals the chance floor. This is a ceiling, not a failure to replicate.
2. MNLI: the model never answered "maybe" (neutral) in 800 prompts. "maybe" reached the top 10 first tokens in 413 of them, at most p=0.22. On the 37 neutral items it put a mean p=0.73 on "no". With a third of the items out of reach, accuracy sits near 58% and the order range is at the null.
3. One demo set is thin. The paper's 5.9-13.3 pt is a mean over 10 sets; our number is one set and is not comparable to it.

Deviations from the paper: chat template instead of raw completion; the label is read from the top-10 logprobs of the first assistant token (case-folded, leading space stripped). Argmax matches label-restricted scoring whenever at least one label is in the top 10, which held for all 1,600 prompts. One demo set, 100 of 200 items, FP8 weights.

Determinism: two runner processes overlapped by accident and scored 8 prompts twice. All 8 pairs gave the same answer. `W005-rows.jsonl` keeps the first of each; 7 stray set-1 rows are dropped.

Re-run: clone the paper repo, `pip install pandas pyarrow`, set the endpoint in `W005-run.py`, `SETS=0 N_ITEMS=100 python3 W005-run.py`, then `python3 W005-compare.py` (paths at the top of each file).
