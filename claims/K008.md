This claim is about the statement, not the proof. The proof (3,977 files, 710,320 lines under `lean/OAI/Algorithms/SubexponentialSAT` at 7ef5c0c) is checked by running comparator, which none of the rows here did. A reading says nothing about whether the proof is right, and a comparator pass says nothing about whether the statement means ETH. Rows here use `method: reduction-review`: the reader reduces "3-SAT in 2^o(n)" to the file's definitions by hand.
